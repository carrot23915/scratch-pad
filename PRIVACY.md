# Privacy Policy for Scratch Pad Portable

**Last updated:** 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Scratch Pad Portable** (“the App”) handles information when you use the Windows application.

Scratch Pad Portable is a local notes tool. It is designed so that **your notes stay on your device**. The developer does not operate a Scratch Pad Portable account system, cloud sync service, or analytics backend for this App.

## 1. Who we are

Scratch Pad Portable is published by the independent developer associated with the GitHub account **carrot23915** (the “Developer”).

## 2. Information the App stores on your device

The App may store the following **only on your computer**:

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Note text and tab names | Let you write and keep multi-tab notes | Local app data folder (`scratchpad.json` under the Windows app data directory used by Electron) |
| Active tab index and related UI state | Restore your session when you reopen the App | Same local file |
| Exported / “Close and save” text files | Create a backup file you requested | Your **Downloads** folder (filename like `Scratch Pad Portable Export DD-MM-YYYY.txt`) |
| Files you choose to import | Restore notes from a previous export | Read only from the file path you select; imported text is then stored locally as notes |

The Developer **does not receive** this content. It is not uploaded to the Developer’s servers because the App does not send your notes to the Developer.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The App does **not** intentionally collect, transmit, or sell:

- Your note contents
- Personal identity information (name, email, address, phone)
- Account credentials (the App has no login)
- Precise location
- Contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics, telemetry, or crash reports operated by the Developer
- Payment or financial information

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

## 4. Internet and network access

Scratch Pad Portable’s features (notes, tabs, import, export / save to Downloads) work **offline** and do not require you to sign in or connect to a Developer-operated service.

If you download the App from the **Microsoft Store**, Microsoft may process information according to Microsoft’s own policies (for example Store account, downloads, and Store-related diagnostics). That processing is controlled by Microsoft, not by Scratch Pad Portable’s note-taking features. See [Microsoft’s Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/).

## 5. How your data is used

Data stored by the App is used only to:

- Display and edit your notes
- Persist notes between sessions on the same device
- Export or import notes when **you** choose those actions
- Show confirmation dialogs when closing the App or a tab

## 6. Sharing of data

The Developer does **not** sell, rent, or share your notes with third parties.

Your notes may become accessible to others only if **you** share them yourself (for example by sending an exported `.txt` file, using device backups, sync folders, screen sharing, or giving someone access to your PC).

## 7. Data retention and deletion

- Local notes remain on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App’s local data, uninstall the App, or remove the files manually.
- Exported files in Downloads remain until you delete them.
- Because the Developer does not host your notes, there is no Developer-side copy to delete. Uninstalling the App and deleting local/export files removes the App’s copies on that device (Windows may keep Store-related metadata under Microsoft’s control).

## 8. Children’s privacy

Scratch Pad Portable is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require accounts or personal data to function.

## 9. Security

Notes are stored as ordinary files on your device. Protection depends on your device account, disk encryption (for example BitLocker), and physical/access controls you use. The App does not add its own end-to-end encryption layer for note files.

## 10. International users

Processing of your notes happens locally on your device. The Developer does not transfer your note content to Developer servers. If you use Microsoft Store services, Microsoft may process data in other regions under its policies.

## 11. Your choices

You can:

- Use the App without creating an account
- Export or delete notes at any time
- Decline to export when closing (choose “Close without saving”)
- Uninstall the App and delete `scratchpad.json` / export files to remove local copies

The “Last updated” date will change, and the updated policy will be posted at this location. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy for that version of the App.
